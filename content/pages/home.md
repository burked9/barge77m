# 77M Vessel Archive & Captain's Logbook

Welcome to the **77M Vessel Archive**, a dedicated logbook and historical repository celebrating canal boat **77M**, historic barges, steam tugs, and the floating heritage of the Irish Inland Waterways.

> *"This is the Captain's Logbook and Restoration record of my very own Canal Boat, 77.M! I will be using this site to log all of the journeys taken since purchasing the barge over twelve months ago, track ongoing journeys, upload photographs of seasonal restoration work, and record the boat's long and detailed history as one of the last workboats—and then dredgers—of the Irish Inland Waterways."*

---

## History & Heritage Highlights

Built in **1937** by the **Ringsend Dockyard Company** in Dublin as a *"McMillen boat"* (61 ft 9 in x 13 ft 3 in), **77M** is one of the last surviving Grand Canal carrying boats and dredgers. 

From her commercial carrying years under CIE to her service as a Priestman dredger, and her 2014 purchase at auction by skipper **Daniel Burke**, her story spans nearly nine decades of Irish maritime history. She is currently undergoing active restoration at **Church Bay on Lough Derg in County Clare**.
