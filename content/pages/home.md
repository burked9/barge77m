# 77M Workboat Archive & Community Fleet

Welcome to the **77M Vessel Archive**, a dedicated repository celebrating the history, engineering, and community legacy of the 77M class workboats and barges.

Over the years, these vessels have served vital operational roles while acting as platforms for community fundraisers, non-profit events, and historical preservation.

Explore our [Fleet Overview]({filename}/pages/fleet.html) or learn more about [Hibernia]({filename}/pages/hibernia.md).
