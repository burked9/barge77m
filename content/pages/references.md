# Publications, References & Archival Sources

A collection of historical references, yard build records, newspaper archives, and oral history accounts for 77M, Little Knocknagow, and Irish inland waterways craft.

## Reference Bibliography

*   **Ringsend Dockyard Company Build Records (1937):** Technical specifications for 77M, 78M, and 79M McMillen boats.
*   **Lanarkshire Steel Co Ltd Markings:** Raised stern plate inscriptions on *Little Knocknagow*.
*   **Waterways Ireland Maintenance & Auction Records (2014):** WI Depot Fenniscourt Lock preservation and 2014 auction history.
*   **Historical Accounts & Regional Histories:**
    *   Dr. Patrick C. Power — *History of Carrick-on-Suir & River Suir Carrying Trade*.
    *   Michael Coady — *The Nationalist* (August 22, 1998) accounts of *Little Knocknagow* in Carrick Town Pond.
    *   Jack O'Neill — Waterford railway and lighterage historical records.
    *   Theo Harris, William O'Callaghan, Sean Barrett, and Richard Miller oral accounts & restoration logs (1975–2004).
